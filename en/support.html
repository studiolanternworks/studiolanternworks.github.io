Support

Help with Studio Lanternworks games

Need help with one of our games? Write to us at support@studiolanternworks.com.

For a faster answer

If you can, include:

  • the name of the game;
  • your device and its iOS or Android version;
  • what happened and, if you can, the steps to make it happen again;
  • for a purchase: the date and the store (App Store or Google Play).

Never send us your password or payment details. We will never ask for them.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases in our games are processed by Apple’s App Store or by Google Play. Refund requests go to the store where the purchase was made; write to us if you need help finding your way.

Response time

We usually reply within a few business days. For anything else, write to hello@studiolanternworks.com.
