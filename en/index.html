THE STUDIO

The joy of discovery.

A picture that hides a detail. A puzzle that clicks after a pause. A place you understand better by exploring it, a world you suddenly see differently. That kind of discovery is what makes us want to make games.

Studio Lanternworks inc. is a small independent video game studio, founded in 2026 in Montréal, Québec.

Our illustrations and music are made with the help of AI tools, then chosen, refined and put together by us.
