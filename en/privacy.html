Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Studio Lanternworks inc. handles personal information related to this website and to the e-mails you send us. It will be extended to cover our games before they are released.

In short

  • This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms.
  • Nothing on it loads from another site.
  • If you write to us, your e-mail is used only to reply to you.
  • We never sell or rent personal information.

Website hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). When the site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor’s IP address for security purposes, under its own privacy statement. We have no access to that data, which may be processed outside Québec.

E-mail

Our @studiolanternworks.com addresses are forwarded by our domain registrar (Porkbun, United States) to our mailbox (Gmail, by Google). If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply and follow up on your request. We keep them as long as that follow-up requires, then delete them. These services may process your messages outside Québec.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent to its use. Write to the person in charge below; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information

The President of Studio Lanternworks inc.
E-mail: hello@studiolanternworks.com

Our governance

Only the studio’s management has access to the personal information we receive. We do not share it with anyone unless the law requires it. Every confidentiality incident is recorded in a register; if it presents a risk of serious injury, we promptly notify the Commission d’accès à l’information and the people concerned. Requests and complaints are handled by the person in charge.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy here, with its date.
